Canopy Rush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Canopy Rush is an arcade game published by Childhood Technology Ltd under the application identifier com.childhood.canopyrush. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the App Processes

Canopy Rush does not require an account, name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details to play.

Game progress such as best score, settings, and completed expedition goals is stored locally on the device.

Advertising

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). When enabled, the advertising SDK may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction events, and diagnostic information according to Google's applicable policies.

This information may be used for purposes including ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Ads are not shown during active gameplay. Native ads may appear below results on larger landscape displays, and app-open ads may appear on eligible returns to the menu.

Optional rewarded and rewarded-interstitial ads may offer one revive after a run ends. Full-screen advertisements at run transitions are frequency capped.

Where required, the app presents an advertising consent form and provides available advertising privacy choices in Settings.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please see Google's Privacy Policy .

iOS Advertising

Advertising in the iOS release is currently disabled.

Data Sharing and Retention

Google may process information through its advertising services under Google's applicable privacy policies.

Locally saved game progress remains on the device unless the player removes the app, clears the app's storage, or the operating system otherwise removes the data.

The publisher does not operate a player-account database for Canopy Rush.

Children

Canopy Rush is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law.

The publisher's Google Play target-audience settings, Google Mobile Ads configuration, and consent handling are intended to remain consistent with the countries and audiences where the app is distributed.

Your Choices

Players can reset locally stored game data through the operating system's app-storage controls.

Advertising personalization settings can be managed through available device and Google account settings.

Where applicable, advertising privacy choices can also be reviewed through the privacy controls provided by the app.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, its features, or its service providers change. The latest version will display its effective date.
