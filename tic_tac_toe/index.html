Childhood Technology Ltd · Tic Tac Toe

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Tic Tac Toe is a tic tac toe game by Childhood Technology Ltd.

The current version does not require users to create an account or log in. The app does not operate an app-owned backend API.

2. Data Collection

Tic Tac Toe may collect limited app usage, gameplay, device, diagnostics, advertising, and analytics information through Google Mobile Ads and Firebase Analytics when those services are enabled.

This information may be used to understand app performance, improve gameplay, measure advertising impact, and serve advertisements.

Advertising consent is requested where required before advertisements are initialized, and privacy choices can be reviewed from the app's Settings.

Firebase Analytics collection can also be turned off from Settings.

3. Local Settings and Game Data

The app saves certain information locally on your device so that preferences and recent gameplay information can be maintained between sessions.

  • Sound settings
  • Vibration settings
  • Recent match history
  • Basic app-open counters
  • Completed match summaries

Current match scores are kept during your local play session. Current match scores are not transmitted as part of the app's own gameplay storage.

4. Gameplay Analytics

When Firebase Analytics is configured, analytics events may include non-personal gameplay and app-usage information such as game mode, match format, round result, move count, difficulty, and advertising lifecycle status.

Analytics collection can be turned off from the app's Settings.

5. Advertising

Tic Tac Toe may use Google Mobile Ads for limited interstitial advertising when AdMob is enabled.

Ad requests and ad interactions may be processed by Google Mobile Ads. Google may process information such as device or other identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related information according to Google's applicable policies.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google's Privacy Policy

6. Analytics

Tic Tac Toe may use Firebase Analytics when the required Firebase configuration is enabled.

Firebase Analytics may receive gameplay, retention, app-usage, and advertising lifecycle events to help understand app performance, gameplay behavior, retention, and advertising impact.

Firebase Analytics is enabled only when the required Firebase configuration is provided. Analytics collection can be turned off from Settings.

For more information about Firebase's privacy practices, please visit:

Firebase Privacy and Security

7. Support Messages

If you choose to use the Send feedback feature, the message you write is sent to the app's configured Formspree support endpoint.

The feedback feature only sends your message when you explicitly tap Send.

Please do not include sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, or other confidential information in a support message.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising
  • Firebase Analytics for analytics and measurement
  • Formspree for user-submitted feedback

These services are operated by their respective providers and may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The app does not operate an app-owned backend API.

9. Permissions and Device Access

The current app does not request access to:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • SMS
  • Call logs
  • Files
  • Health information
  • Nearby devices

Internet and network-state permissions are used for supported advertising and analytics services.

10. Children

Tic Tac Toe is not currently configured as a child-directed app.

If the target audience or child-directed status changes, this Privacy Policy and the app's applicable Google Play store declarations will be updated before release.

11. Accounts and Login

Tic Tac Toe does not require users to create an account or log in.

All game features are accessible without an account.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Local settings and completed match information remain on the device until they are removed by the user.

Local application data can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads, Firebase Analytics, or Formspree is subject to the applicable policies and retention practices of those services.

13. Changes to This Policy

If future versions add purchases, cloud save, login, crash reporting, online features, or other data-collecting services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what data is collected and why.

This Privacy Policy may also be updated when the app's advertising, analytics, support, or data-handling configuration changes.

When this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same public URL with a new effective date.
