1. Information handled

Game data stored on your device

The Game stores puzzle progress, found words, completed and unlocked levels, tutorial status, daily field-note progress, collected stamps, selected atlas style and avatar, rewarded-assistance usage, and sound, music, haptics and motion preferences locally on your device. These records allow you to continue playing and keep your choices between sessions.

The Game does not require registration or a player account, and it does not directly upload these saved game records to a server operated by us. Your operating system may include local app data in device backups according to your device settings and the backup provider's policy.

Advertising and consent information

On supported Android devices, the Game uses Google's advertising and consent services. Depending on consent, device settings and the services in use, Google may collect or receive IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information. An IP address may indicate an approximate location. Consent services also handle your privacy choices.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location for its puzzle features.

Support messages and email

If you choose to send a message through the in-game Support form, the Game sends the text you enter and the installed app name, used in the message subject, to Formspree for delivery to our support team. The form does not require your name or email address. Any personal information you include in the message is supplied voluntarily. Formspree may also process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure its service.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you provide. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information in a support request.

2. How information is used

  • To operate the Game, save progress, restore an unfinished puzzle and remember settings.
  • To manage daily goals, earned visual styles and optional rewarded assistance.
  • To show and measure advertising, manage consent, prevent advertising fraud and maintain the reliability of advertising services.
  • To receive and respond to support or privacy requests and investigate reported problems.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and protect the security of our services.

The Game does not include a separate developer-operated gameplay analytics or cloud account service. Advertising providers may perform their own measurement and diagnostics as described in their policies.

3. Advertising and consent

The Android Game may show app-open advertisements and offers an optional rewarded advertisement for assistance. Watching a rewarded advertisement is not required for ordinary puzzle progression.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where required and to determine whether advertisements may be requested. Where Google requires an ongoing privacy entry point, you can find Privacy options in the Game's Settings. Available choices and whether advertisements are personalized depend on your region, consent and provider configuration. Non-personalized or limited advertisements may still involve some data processing.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising. Android also provides advertising privacy controls in device settings.

4. Service providers and sharing

Information is disclosed as needed to the services used by the Game:

  • Google advertising and consent services: for ad delivery, consent management, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention.
  • Formspree and email providers: to transmit, store and deliver messages that you choose to send to support.

Read Formspree's Privacy Policy for its handling of form submissions and technical information. Provider processing is subject to applicable agreements, settings and privacy policies.

We do not send your saved puzzle progress to advertising providers or sell your support messages. Ad-related disclosures may be considered targeted advertising or "sharing" under some privacy laws. Where applicable, use the available consent controls or contact us about your choices.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or when necessary to protect people, rights or the security of our services.

5. Storage and retention

Local game records remain on your device until app data is cleared, the Game is removed, or the operating system otherwise removes the data. Backups may remain subject to your backup settings. The Game may retain a separate local recovery save to avoid overwriting an unreadable earlier save.

Support communications are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal or security obligations. The exact period depends on the request and provider settings. You can contact us to request deletion.

Advertising and consent data held by Google, and technical data held by other providers, follow those providers' retention practices. Removing the Game does not automatically delete information already held by providers.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Play offline: core puzzles and local progress work without an internet connection. Advertising and sending support messages require connectivity.
  • Manage advertising: use Privacy options when available, together with your device's advertising privacy controls. Declining a rewarded-ad offer does not prevent ordinary play.
  • Remove local records: use the operating system's app-data controls or remove the Game. This can permanently remove progress, rewards and settings. Manage device backups separately if enabled.
  • Request deletion of support information: email shuvo4727@gmail.com with enough information to locate your correspondence. Do not send additional sensitive information. We may need to verify the request before acting.

Because there is no player account or developer cloud save, we generally cannot identify or remotely retrieve your locally stored game records. Requests about information held independently by a provider may also need to be directed to that provider.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain targeted advertising or data-sharing activities. You may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Where applicable, processing relies on your consent, responding to your request for support, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or compliance with legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Contact us using the email below to exercise a right. Applicable legal exceptions, identity verification and the information we actually hold may affect how a request can be fulfilled.

8. Children's privacy

Word Trail Expedition is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through our support feature. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps to delete that information.

A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of online support and third-party services. Additional protections or age thresholds may apply under local law.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled. In-game support messages are transmitted using HTTPS. However, no storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google, Formspree and email providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those countries may have different data protection laws. Applicable transfer protections and further details are described in the providers' policies and agreements.

This policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or tracking cookies of its own. When it is hosted online, the hosting service may process ordinary technical access logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to deliver and secure the page.
