Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Childhood Technology Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Twin Tower Drop. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, view advertisements, contact support, or visit this policy page.

For privacy questions or requests, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com.

At a glance: You do not need an account to play. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. The Android game uses Google AdMob for advertising, and messages you choose to send through in-game Support are processed through Formspree. These services may handle personal information as described below.

1. Information we handle

Local game information. The game stores your current build, scores, bridge records, tutorial progress, light shards, daily commissions, district restoration, color choices, and audio, haptic, and motion preferences on your device. It also keeps local counts of ad offers and rewards to support recovery features and prevent duplicate grants. The current game does not upload this local save or these counters to a game server operated by us, and does not provide cloud save or account-based synchronization.

Support information. If you use in-game Support, we receive the message you submit and an automatically generated subject identifying the installed app’s display name. The form does not require your name or email address. Anything you include in your message, including contact details, is submitted voluntarily. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send.

Service and connection information. Advertising and support providers may receive information such as your IP address and technical request data when your device connects to their services. Advertising-related information is described in the next section. The current game does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

2. Advertising and Google services

The Android game integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads may include app-open ads, advertisements at breaks between runs, and optional rewarded advertisements. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent you from starting a new run or earning ordinary game progression.

Google’s advertising services may collect and share IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. This information can be used to deliver and measure advertising, maintain service functionality, and prevent fraud. Processing depends on the SDK, your device settings, your region, and applicable consent choices. Advertising partners may process information under their own privacy policies; ad-supported play is not a promise that no information leaves your device.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where required. Where available, open Settings → Privacy choices to review or change those choices. This entry appears when Google requires it for your location or consent state. Non-personalized ads may still involve processing for delivery, security, and measurement.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

3. Why information is used

  • To operate the game, remember your choices, and restore your saved progress.
  • To track local objectives and correctly apply earned rewards and recovery credits.
  • To deliver advertising and respect applicable privacy choices.
  • To respond to support requests, troubleshoot problems you report, and protect services against misuse.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and handle legitimate legal requests.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the activity: providing a service you request, legitimate interests in maintaining and protecting that service, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required. You may withdraw consent for processing based on consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Who may receive information

Google AdMob and its advertising partners handle information for advertising and related privacy services as described above.

Formspree processes in-game support submissions and may handle connection metadata for delivery, security, and abuse prevention. Its practices are explained in the Formspree Privacy Policy. Email providers also process correspondence when you contact us or when a support submission is delivered by email.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or service security. We do not make your local game save publicly available. Advertising-related transfers may be treated as “sharing” or similar activity under some privacy laws; the choices available to you depend on your location and the advertising service.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local progress remains in the app’s storage until you remove it using your device’s app-data controls or uninstall the app, subject to your operating system’s backup and restore behavior. Your device or platform may retain backups according to its own settings. Removing local data can permanently erase your progress.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to address your request, maintain relevant support records, protect against abuse, or meet legal obligations. Retention by Google, Formspree, email providers, and platform backup services is governed by their own policies and any applicable service settings. Deleting the app does not automatically delete information already held by those services.

You can request deletion of support information we hold by emailing us. Include enough information to identify the correspondence; do not send sensitive identification documents unless a secure and necessary verification method has been agreed. We cannot retrieve or delete a device-only save remotely.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Advertising choices: Use the game’s Privacy choices entry when available, your device’s advertising/privacy settings, and Google My Ad Center where applicable. Available controls vary by device and account.
  • Offline play: Core gameplay works without an internet connection. Ads and support submissions require connectivity; previously submitted information is not removed by going offline.
  • Local data: Use your device’s app-storage controls to remove game data, taking account of any platform backups.
  • Support: You can play without submitting a support message or providing an email address.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise rights concerning information we control, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify a request and will handle it as required by applicable law. For information independently controlled by a third-party service, its own privacy-request channels may also be necessary.

7. Children’s privacy

Twin Tower Drop is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or a higher age where required by local law. Children should not include identifying or sensitive information in support messages. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

8. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle. The game submits in-app support requests over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and local saves may be accessible through your device or its backups.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States, where privacy laws may differ. Applicable safeguards and transfer arrangements depend on the provider, the processing involved, and applicable law. Consult the linked provider policies for further information.

9. This page and external links

Its hosting provider may process routine connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the page. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.
