1. Who we are

Childhood Technology Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Cable Garden. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game or contact us about it.

Cable Garden does not require an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your puzzle progress. Advertising providers and services you choose to visit have their own privacy practices, explained below.

2. Information the game handles

Game data stored on your device

The game saves information needed to continue your experience, including:

  • Your current puzzle, lamp positions, move history, completed gardens, best firefly scores, and unlocked content.
  • Tutorial completion, lesson progress, and the saved puzzle you return to after replaying a lesson.
  • Daily task progress, task date markers, reward claims, stamps, and your selected celebration lighting.
  • Earned hint credits and any revealed hint awaiting your next move.
  • Music and sound-effect preferences and volumes, haptics, and reduced-motion settings.

We use this information to run the game, restore your session, apply your preferences, and keep local rewards and progress consistent. The game does not send this saved puzzle data to a developer-operated backend.

Advertising and consent information

When advertising services are used, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK and consent tools process information separately from your local puzzle save. Depending on your settings, region, and the advertising service’s configuration, this includes:

CategoryExamples and purpose
Network informationIP address, which can be used to estimate a general location and deliver ads.
Device identifiersAdvertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers available to the SDK, subject to applicable device controls.
InteractionsApp launches, ad views, taps, and other interactions used for advertising and measurement.
DiagnosticsPerformance, errors, and SDK diagnostics used to maintain services, measure performance, and prevent fraud.
Privacy choicesConsent status and related choices used to determine which advertising requests are permitted.

The game does not request access to your precise GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library. An approximate location inferred by an advertising service from an IP address is different from precise GPS access.

We have not added a separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service to Cable Garden. This does not mean advertising services collect no measurement or diagnostic information.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your inquiry, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The Android version of Cable Garden uses Google AdMob. It may show app-open ads at eligible Home opportunities and optional rewarded ads for hints. Ordinary puzzle play, undo, retry, and normal progression do not require watching a rewarded ad.

Advertising services use information to deliver and measure ads, support their services, and detect fraud or abuse. Ad personalization depends on applicable consent, device settings, and Google’s policies. Choosing non-personalized or limited ads does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.

Where applicable, Google’s consent tools present privacy choices before advertising requests are permitted. When a privacy-options entry is required, you can revisit it in Settings → Privacy options. This entry is displayed when required by the consent service.

For details about Google’s practices and controls, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google’s explanation of advertising technologies
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures

Opening an ad or external link may take you to another service. That service’s policy applies to information you provide there. We do not control the privacy practices of advertisers or linked websites.

4. How information is shared

We do not upload or sell your locally saved puzzle progress. Advertising-related information is collected and shared through Google’s advertising services as described above and in Google’s policies.

If you contact us, email service providers process the correspondence needed to deliver and manage your message. We may disclose information we hold when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address fraud, security problems, or protect people’s rights.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Advertising choices: use Settings → Privacy options when available to review or change the choices offered by the consent service. Withdrawing consent affects future processing that depends on that consent.
  • Device controls: your operating system may let you reset or delete your advertising identifier or adjust advertising preferences. Available controls depend on your device and operating-system version.
  • Optional rewards: you can decline a rewarded ad and continue ordinary gameplay. Ads and consent services require a network connection; normal puzzles can be played offline.
  • Local data: clear the app’s storage through your device settings to remove its locally saved game data. This also removes your progress and preferences.
  • Contact us: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or request a copy of information we hold. Send requests to the email below. We may need information reasonably necessary to verify and handle your request.

We cannot directly retrieve a puzzle save that remains only on your device, or directly delete information independently held by Google. We can explain the controls available to you; requests about Google-held information may also need to be submitted through Google’s privacy tools. Where applicable, you may raise concerns with your local data-protection authority.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local game data stays in the app’s storage until it is changed, cleared, or removed by your device. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but device or operating-system backup features may retain or restore a copy according to your settings. Cable Garden does not provide its own cloud-save account or cross-device synchronization service.

Daily task counters reset as part of gameplay, while permanent progress and earned collection rewards remain saved. Resetting a daily counter does not erase other saved progress.

If you contact us, we retain correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, keep necessary records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Google’s retention periods for advertising and consent information are governed by its policies. Clearing or uninstalling the game does not automatically delete data already held by those services.

7. Security and international processing

Local saves use the storage provided by your device’s operating system. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK user data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No device, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Advertising and email providers may process information on servers outside your country. Their privacy policies explain their processing locations and any applicable safeguards. We do not promise that all information remains in a particular country.

8. Children’s privacy

Cable Garden is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Parents and guardians should review device controls and the advertising provider’s privacy information before allowing a child to use an ad-supported app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its information-handling practices change. The revised policy will show an updated date on this page. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent for a change, we will provide it.
