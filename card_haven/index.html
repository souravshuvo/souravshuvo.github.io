1. Who we are

Childhood Technology Ltd. ("we," "us" or "our") provides Card Haven, an Android solitaire game. This policy explains the information handled through the game, our support channels and this policy page.

For questions or privacy requests, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Game data stored on your device

Card Haven stores your current and suspended deals, move history, tutorial progress, personal statistics, completed challenges, daily goals and claims, Insight balance, earned rewards, selected appearance, audio and accessibility settings, and advertising cooldown or reward records locally. An unfinished support-message draft may also be saved locally.

The game may keep local recovery copies of an older or damaged save to help preserve progress. We do not operate a game-account or cloud-save service, and our gameplay code does not upload your saved boards or profile to us. Device or operating-system backup services may separately back up app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and consent information

Card Haven integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google's consent tools. Depending on device settings, consent and service configuration, Google may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), advertising or other device/app identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

See Google Mobile Ads data disclosures and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Information you send to support

If you use in-app support, the game sends the message you enter and an automatically generated subject containing the app's display name to Formspree, which processes and routes the submission to our support channel. The support form does not require your name, email address or phone number. Information you include voluntarily becomes part of your message.

Formspree may also process network information, such as your IP address and request metadata, to deliver and protect its service. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, through our email provider. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Local diagnostics and device access

The game may keep a limited recent list of gameplay events in memory and write diagnostic messages to device logs for troubleshooting. Our local event system does not send those logs to a developer-operated analytics server. Advertising SDK diagnostics are handled separately as described above.

Card Haven does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location for gameplay. Copying a deal code uses your device clipboard only when you select the copy action.

3. How information is used

  • To run the game, restore sessions, save your preferences and maintain progression.
  • To track daily goals, grant earned rewards and prevent duplicate claims.
  • To provide advertising, remember consent choices, measure ad performance and prevent abuse.
  • To deliver support messages, investigate reported issues and respond when you provide a way to contact you.
  • To maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely, as applicable, on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in support and security, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent where required, including for relevant advertising activities.

4. Advertising and your choices

The game may display rewarded advertisements and limited advertisements outside active play. Choosing a rewarded ad is optional. Declining that offer does not prevent ordinary gameplay, but it does not disable every other ad or all advertising-related processing.

Where required, Google's consent messages let you review available advertising choices. You can revisit them through Settings → Privacy choices when that option is available in the game. Changing or withdrawing consent affects subsequent processing and does not undo processing that was lawful before the change.

You can also manage or delete your advertising ID and available ad-privacy settings in Android settings. The available controls depend on your Android version and device. Google explains its advertising controls at My Ad Center.

The game requests ads only when Google's consent tools indicate that ads may be requested. Depending on the choices available in your region, limited or non-personalized ads may still involve processing for delivery, security and measurement.

5. When information is shared

Information is handled by service providers involved in the features you use:

  • Google AdMob and its advertising services: advertising, consent, measurement and fraud prevention, as described above.
  • Formspree: processing and routing in-app support submissions. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Our email provider: receiving and managing support or privacy correspondence.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect people, our services and legal rights. We do not sell your saved game data or support messages. Advertising-related disclosures may be considered “sharing” or similar activity under some privacy laws; use the available advertising controls or contact us about your rights.

External advertisements and links can lead to services with their own privacy practices. Their policies apply when you interact with those services.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data: game saves, settings, reward records, drafts and recovery copies remain in app storage until replaced or removed. To erase them, open Android settings, select Card Haven, and clear the app's storage/data. This resets your local progress and cannot be undone through our game service.

Uninstalling or restoring the app can interact with operating-system backups. Manage any device or cloud backups separately through the relevant provider's controls; clearing local app data does not itself delete a provider's backup.

Support correspondence: we retain messages for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. Providers may retain separate records or backups under their policies and applicable obligations.

Advertising data: Google determines retention for information it processes under its policies and available controls. Deleting your local game progress does not automatically delete information already held by Google or Formspree.

No Card Haven account is created, so there is no game account to delete.

7. Security and international processing

The in-app support feature uses HTTPS, and Google's advertising SDK uses encrypted transport for its data transmissions. We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no device, transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google, Formspree and our email provider may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Applicable protections and transfer arrangements depend on the provider, destination and relevant law. Their linked privacy policies provide additional information.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent, or to opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. These rights are subject to applicable law and exceptions.

Send requests to shuvo4727@gmail.com with “Card Haven privacy request” in the subject. Describe your request and the information involved. We may need reasonable information to verify that the request concerns you. We cannot retrieve a local-only save from your device.

For information held independently by Google or Formspree, you may also use their privacy controls or contact them directly. You may have the right to raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

9. Children's privacy

Card Haven is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our support channels. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps to remove it.
