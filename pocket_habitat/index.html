Privacy Policy

Childhood Technology Ltd.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Childhood Technology Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") provides Pocket Habitat. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, use its support feature, or contact us about privacy.

At a glance: No account is needed. Your puzzle progress and preferences are saved on your device. The Android app uses Google AdMob for advertising, and support messages are delivered through Formspree. These services handle information separately from your local game save.

1. Information handled by the app

Game progress and preferences

Pocket Habitat stores puzzle moves, completed and unlocked habitats, scores, tutorial progress, hint balances, daily task and reward history, launch and advertising timing information, and audio, haptic, and motion preferences on your device. This information lets the game resume your session, apply rewards, and remember your choices.

The game does not provide its own account system, cloud save service, or developer-operated server that receives your game save. Device or operating-system backup and restore features may handle app data under your device settings and the platform provider's policies.

Support and privacy messages

If you choose Settings → Contact Support, the app sends the message you write and a subject containing the installed app's name to Formspree for delivery to our support team. It does not automatically attach your puzzle save. The form has no separate name or email field; any personal details you put in the message are sent with it.

Formspree may also process connection information, such as an IP address and request timing, to deliver and protect its service. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Include a reply address in an in-app message only if you want us to be able to contact you.

Device access and analytics

The app's gameplay does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location. The current app does not integrate a separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service. Advertising services can nevertheless process their own interaction and diagnostic information, as described below.

2. Advertising and Google's services

The Android app integrates Google AdMob to show app-open ads and optional rewarded ads. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent normal puzzle play or free retries. Advertising requests may occur even if you do not choose a rewarded offer.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, advertising or other device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google may infer an approximate location from an IP address. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, service operation, and fraud prevention; personalization depends on applicable settings, eligibility, and consent requirements.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google and advertising partners handle their information under their own applicable policies.

The current app has no in-app advertising-consent preferences screen. Device advertising controls are separate from any consent rights you may have. Reading this policy or using the game does not, by itself, constitute consent where consent is required.

3. How information is used

  • To provide gameplay, retain local progress, and remember settings.
  • To calculate and save eligible hint rewards and daily task claims.
  • To deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.
  • To answer support or privacy inquiries and investigate reported problems.
  • To protect the app and services against abuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the activity: providing requested services, legitimate interests such as security and support where permitted, legal obligations, or consent where required. A provider's own policy explains its processing and legal bases.

4. Who receives information

Information is shared with the services needed for the features you use: Google AdMob for advertising, Formspree for support submissions, and the email services used to handle correspondence. Our support team can access messages delivered to us. We do not operate a separate business selling your puzzle-save information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or a valid legal request, protect rights and safety, or investigate abuse. If ownership of the app changes, relevant information may be transferred subject to applicable law and any required notice.

Formspree's processing is described in its Privacy Policy. Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, where privacy laws may differ. Their policies explain their international processing practices.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data: Your save remains on your device until app data is removed or replaced. You can clear the app's storage using available device controls or uninstall the app. This can permanently remove progress, settings, and unused hints. Operating-system backups may retain or restore a copy; manage those separately through your platform's backup settings.

Support correspondence: We retain messages and any replies for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, maintain relevant support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Messages may exist in Formspree and our support mailbox. You can request deletion using the contact details below; we may need enough information to locate the message and verify the request.

Advertising information: Google and its partners apply their own retention and deletion practices. Clearing the game's storage does not automatically delete information already held by those providers. Use their privacy tools or contact them for requests about information they control.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Play core puzzles offline. Ads and sending support messages need a network connection.
  • Decline optional rewarded offers and continue normal play.
  • Use your device's advertising privacy controls, where available, and Google's ad controls. These controls do not necessarily prevent all ad-related processing.
  • Choose whether to send support messages and what details to include.
  • Remove local game data through device storage or uninstall controls.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Contact us to make a request concerning information we hold. Please identify Pocket Habitat and describe your request; do not send an identity document unless a justified verification step is separately explained. We cannot remotely retrieve a device-only puzzle save that has never been sent to us. There is no Pocket Habitat account to delete.

7. Security

The in-app support feature sends messages over HTTPS. Local saves use the app's device storage; they are not a secure vault for sensitive information. We take reasonable care when handling support records, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and avoid including unnecessary personal information in messages.

8. Children's privacy

Pocket Habitat is not directed to children under 13, or below the applicable age of digital consent in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required. The presence of wildlife artwork does not indicate a child-directed advertising service.

9. External links and this policy page

Ads and links may open services operated by other organizations. Their privacy policies apply to their services. This policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising code, embedded third-party media, or externally loaded fonts. When this page is hosted online, the hosting provider may process ordinary connection logs, such as IP address, browser information, and access time.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The updated policy will show a revised date on this page. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required by applicable law.
