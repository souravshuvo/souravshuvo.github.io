Gate Squad

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Gate Squad: Survival Shooter is an arcade survival shooter published by Childhood Technology Ltd.

The core game is designed to work without an internet connection. The game does not require an online connection for its core gameplay.

Game Progress and Settings

Gate Squad allows players to progress through up to 150 levels, unlock stages, earn stars, and replay completed levels.

Game progress is saved on the player's device so that campaign progress can be continued later.

The game also allows players to adjust sound and haptic settings.

Advertising

Gate Squad contains advertisements.

Optional rewarded revives and other advertisements require an internet connection.

Advertising services may process information required to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising. The specific information processed may depend on the advertising SDKs and configuration used by the released version of the app.

Offline Gameplay

The core game works without an internet connection. Players can control their squad, choose math gates, fight enemy waves, progress through levels, and battle bosses while playing the core offline game experience.

Some advertising-related features, including optional rewarded revives and other ads, require an internet connection.

Information We Collect

Gate Squad is designed primarily around local gameplay and on-device campaign progress.

This Privacy Policy does not state that the game collects names, email addresses, contacts, photos, audio recordings, precise location, or other personal profile information unless such collection is specifically required by a third-party service used in the released version.

Third-Party Services

Advertising features may use third-party advertising services. Information processed by those services is subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

Children's Privacy

Data Security
