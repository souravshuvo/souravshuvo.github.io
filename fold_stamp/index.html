1. Information the app handles

Game data on your device. The app stores puzzle progress, unfinished attempts, collected patterns, best move records, play statistics, tutorial state, daily commissions, preview tickets and settings such as sound, music, haptics and reduced motion. These records support gameplay and resume features. The app does not upload these local game saves to a server operated by us and does not require a player account.

Your operating system may include app data in device backup or transfer features, depending on your device settings. We do not control those operating-system services.

Information you send for support. The in-app support form sends your message and a subject containing the installed app name through Formspree. It does not automatically attach your game save or request a name or email address. Anything personal you include in the message will be part of your submission. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.

Technical information processed by providers. Advertising and support providers may receive information such as your IP address, device or software details, request timing, identifiers and diagnostic information when their services are used. The advertising information is described below.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise device location.

2. Advertising and consent

Fold & Stamp uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads, including eligible app-open ads and optional rewarded ads. Core puzzles can be played without watching rewarded ads.

Google's SDK may automatically collect and share your IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. An IP address can be used to estimate your general location. This information may be used to provide and measure advertising, personalize ads where permitted, and detect fraud or abuse. What is processed depends on consent, device settings and the applicable advertising configuration.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent information and show required consent messages. Where required, an in-app Settings → Privacy choices option lets you review or change your choices. The availability of that option depends on Google's regional requirements. Non-personalized or limited ads may still involve processing technical information.

You can also use your device's advertising privacy settings to manage advertising identifiers and personalization. Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for details about Google's practices and controls.

  • To save and restore your game, settings and earned progress.
  • To process support requests and respond when contact information is available.
  • To provide, measure and protect advertising services, subject to applicable choices and requirements.
  • To maintain service security, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires consent, legitimate interests in support and security where permitted, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent for future consent-based processing through available controls or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be handled by the following providers when their services are used:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: advertising delivery, measurement, consent-related services and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree: delivery and processing of messages submitted through the in-app support form. Formspree may process submission content and technical request information for operation and security. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers: processing and storage of support correspondence sent to our contact address.

We do not offer your support messages for sale. Advertising-related disclosures may be treated as a "sale", "sharing" or targeted advertising under some privacy laws; the rights available to you depend on your location. Use the available advertising controls or contact us to make a privacy request.

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce legal rights or address fraud or security threats. We do not make your local game records publicly visible.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain in the app's storage until that storage is cleared, the app is removed, or your operating system otherwise manages it. Device backups may have separate retention and restoration rules. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete game records that exist only on your device.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request, maintain necessary records, address security issues and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete support information we control. Providers maintain information under their own policies and applicable obligations; deletion of app data does not automatically delete their records.

6. Your privacy choices and rights

  • You can play core puzzles offline. Ads and support submissions require a connection.
  • You can choose whether to submit a support message or send an email. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.
  • You can review available in-app privacy choices and your device's advertising settings.
  • You can clear the app's storage using Android settings or uninstall it to remove locally stored game data. This can permanently remove your progress; device backups may restore it separately.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related uses. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request concerning information we control, email shuvo4727@gmail.com. Describe your request and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant correspondence. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and any required retention obligations.

7. Children's privacy

The game does not request a player's age or date of birth, and we do not ask children to submit personal information through support. Parents and guardians should guide children's use of online services and advertising choices. If you are below the age at which you can provide valid consent in your location, seek a parent or guardian's help before submitting personal information or agreeing to optional data use.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps to remove information we control where required. Advertising services may process technical data as explained in this policy.
