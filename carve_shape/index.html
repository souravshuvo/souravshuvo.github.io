1. Information handled

Game data stored on your device

The game saves your tutorial state, unfinished carving strokes, order progress, best results, ribbons, decoration tokens, owned and selected workbench finishes, daily commission progress and claims, reward-claim state, and sound, vibration and motion preferences.

This information supports local play and is not uploaded by the game to a developer-operated account or cloud-save service. Your operating system or device backup service may separately back up or restore app data according to its settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Android game integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to handle applicable advertising privacy choices. The game may display app-open ads and optional rewarded ads.

When advertising services operate, Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Device, app and advertising identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available;
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, impressions and video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK; and
  • Advertising consent status and related privacy-choice information.

Data use depends on your location, device settings, consent choices and the advertising service’s configuration. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve some data processing. Google describes its SDK’s practices in its Mobile Ads data disclosure and Privacy Policy.

Messages you send to support

If you use the in-game support form, the game sends the message you enter and the installed app’s display name, used in the support subject, to Formspree. Formspree processes the submission for delivery to us and may handle connection metadata, such as IP address and request time, for operating and securing its service.

The form does not automatically attach your saved game or ask for an email address. If you include contact details or other personal information in your message, that information is sent with it. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send, along with the email service’s usual delivery information. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Permissions and separate analytics

The game uses an internet connection for ads and support. Its gameplay code does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise device location. The game does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or account system; advertising providers may perform their own advertising measurement and diagnostics as described above.

2. Why information is used

  • Restore your game, apply settings, track objectives and prevent duplicate local reward claims;
  • Provide and measure advertising, grant earned ad rewards, and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • Remember and apply applicable advertising privacy choices;
  • Handle support requests, troubleshoot issues and communicate with you when contact details are available; and
  • Protect our services and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may be necessary to provide the game or requested support, pursue legitimate interests in security and service operation, comply with legal obligations, or rely on consent where required. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

3. Providers and sharing

Information is shared with providers as needed for the features you use:

  • Google AdMob and associated advertising partners: ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, fraud prevention and privacy-choice handling. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Formspree: submission and delivery of in-game support messages. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers: delivery and storage of support correspondence.

We do not sell your local game saves or support messages. Advertising providers’ use of identifiers for personalized advertising may be treated as “sharing,” “targeted advertising,” or a “sale” under some privacy laws; applicable choices and rights are described below.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect people or our legal rights, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations.

4. Your choices

  • Advertising choices: Use the advertising consent message when presented. Where required and available, open Settings & help → Privacy choices in the game to review or change your preferences.
  • Device advertising controls: Your Android settings may let you reset or delete the advertising ID and manage ad privacy. Availability and menu names vary by Android version. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing.
  • Optional rewards: You can decline a rewarded-ad offer and continue normal play without that extra reward. A rewarded-ad offer does not require you to send a support message or provide contact details.
  • Local data: Clear the game’s storage using your device settings to remove locally stored progress and preferences. Uninstalling generally removes the local app copy; device backups may remain or restore data unless you manage those backups separately.
  • Support: Contacting us is optional. Include only information needed to explain your request.

Clearing local storage or uninstalling does not automatically delete messages already sent to support or data held by advertising providers.

5. Storage and retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is changed or removed through the game, device storage controls or uninstallation, subject to device backup and restore settings. A local recovery copy may be kept to restore a damaged save.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, resolve disputes, protect the service or satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion using the contact address below, subject to applicable exceptions.

Advertising, consent, form-delivery and email providers retain data under their own policies and applicable agreements. Clearing a game save does not reset those providers’ retention periods.

6. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing or processing. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Send requests to shuvo4727@gmail.com, identifying the game and the information or request involved. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your request. We do not need your password or a complete saved game to receive a privacy request.

We cannot remotely identify or erase a save stored only on your device; use the local controls above. Requests concerning data held independently by Google or another provider may also need to be addressed to that provider. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

7. Children’s privacy

We do not ask children to provide a name, email address or other personal details as a condition of playing. Advertising services may process device and interaction information as described in this policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, including through support, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com so we can review the matter and arrange appropriate deletion or other action required by law. Parents and guardians can also manage app access, advertising settings and local data through the device’s controls.

8. Security and international transfers

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled. In-game support requests are sent over HTTPS, and Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. Local game preferences are stored through the device’s app storage mechanisms. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, where privacy rules may differ. Transfers are subject to applicable law and the providers’ relevant privacy terms and safeguards.
