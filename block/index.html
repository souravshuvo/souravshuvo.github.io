16 Breed Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

16 Breed is a strategy board game published by Childhood Technology Ltd under the Android package com.childhood.sixteenbreed. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Game Data

16 Breed does not require an account. Match history, game progress, and settings are stored on your device and are not uploaded by 16 Breed to a server operated by the publisher.

Bluetooth Play

When you choose Bluetooth play, the app may request Nearby devices permission to discover compatible Android devices and establish a direct game connection.

16 Breed does not request device location for Bluetooth gameplay. Game commands sent over Bluetooth are used only to keep both players' matches synchronized.

Advertising and Third-Party Data

16 Breed uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, advertising and app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for purposes including advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Google states that information transmitted by its services is protected using encryption in transit. For more information about Google's privacy practices, please see Google's Privacy Policy .

Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law or regulation, 16 Breed uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising privacy choices before requesting advertisements.

Where a privacy-options entry point is required and available, users may be able to review or change their advertising privacy choices from the app's Settings screen.

Children

16 Breed is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

The publisher's Google Play audience declaration and advertising configuration are intended to remain consistent with the actual distribution and functionality of the app. If the app's target audience or advertising configuration changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

Security and Retention

16 Breed does not operate its own online game server and does not maintain a user account or user profile.

Game data stored locally on your device remains there until it is deleted by you, removed when the app is uninstalled, or otherwise removed by the operating system.

Data processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's applicable security, retention, and deletion practices.

Your Choices

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. You can also manage available device and advertising privacy controls provided by Android, Google, or the app.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights concerning personal data. For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us using the email address below.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, its features, or its service providers change. The latest version will display its effective date.
