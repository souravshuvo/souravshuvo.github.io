Police Theif: Chor Police

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Police Theif: Chor Police handles information when you use the game.

Police Theif: Chor Police is a four-player secret-role game in which players receive hidden roles, use emoji reactions, make suspect decisions, and compete across a 20-round match.

The game can be played solo with bots or with nearby friends using Bluetooth between paired Android phones.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress and game history can be maintained between sessions.

This may include information such as:

  • Completed matches
  • Match wins
  • Scores and round results
  • Local match history
  • Local player profile or progress information
  • Game preferences
  • Cosmetic design selections

The game does not require you to create an online account in order to play.

Locally stored game information can be removed by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Bluetooth Multiplayer

Police Theif: Chor Police supports multiplayer between nearby friends using Bluetooth on paired Android phones.

Bluetooth is used to support the local multiplayer experience, including exchanging the information required for the current game between participating devices.

The game does not provide internet matchmaking or online multiplayer.

Bluetooth functionality is not used by the game for advertising or for tracking your location.

4. Game Information and Secret Roles

During a match, the game manages information required to operate the secret-role gameplay, including player roles, round results, scores, suspect selections, and emoji reactions.

This information is used to operate the current game and provide the gameplay experience.

Secret roles and gameplay information are not intended to be used as a personal profile or for advertising purposes.

5. Advertising

Police Theif: Chor Police contains advertising outside active gameplay.

The game may offer optional rewarded advertisements that unlock cosmetic designs. Watching a rewarded advertisement does not add points to a player's score.

Advertising may be provided through third-party advertising services. These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

The exact information processed by advertising services depends on the advertising SDK configuration used by the production version of the application.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law or by an advertising provider, the game may request consent before certain advertising technologies are used.

Your consent choices may affect the advertisements and advertising technologies available to you.

7. Children's Privacy

Police Theif: Chor Police is a family-friendly game. The game does not require players to create an online account to play.

Advertising and audience settings should be configured according to the final Google Play target-audience declaration and the production advertising configuration.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy concern regarding the use of the game, please contact the developer using the contact information provided below.

8. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored locally by the application remains on the device until it is deleted through Android settings or the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is retained according to the applicable policies and settings of those services.
