Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pen Cricket handles information when you use the Pen Cricket mobile game.

Pen Cricket is an offline pen cricket game featuring practice innings, target chase, offline challenges, local progress, and local Bluetooth multiplayer between nearby paired Android devices.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Pen Cricket stores certain gameplay information locally on your device so that the game can remember your progress and settings.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored gameplay information may include:

  • Best scores
  • Chase wins
  • Match count and recent match history
  • Offline challenge progress
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Sound and haptic preferences

Pen Cricket does not require you to create an account to play the game.

Local gameplay information can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Bluetooth Multiplayer

Pen Cricket supports local Bluetooth multiplayer between nearby paired Android phones.

Bluetooth is used to support the local multiplayer feature. Pen Cricket does not provide internet matchmaking or cloud multiplayer.

Pen Cricket does not use Bluetooth to provide advertising or to track your location.

4. Advertising

Advertising may be provided through Google Mobile Ads when advertising is enabled in the production version of the application.

Ads are intended to appear in appropriate areas such as menus or match results and are not intended to be part of active spinner deliveries.

Advertising services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements. The exact data processed depends on the advertising SDK configuration used in the released version of Pen Cricket.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law or the advertising platform, Pen Cricket may request your consent before certain advertising technologies are used.

Your advertising consent choices may affect which advertisements or advertising technologies are available to you.

The final consent and advertising configuration may vary depending on the version of the application and the user's location.

6. Analytics

The Pen Cricket project includes Firebase Analytics integration. Analytics may be used to understand application usage, diagnose problems, and improve the game.

The specific information collected by Firebase Analytics depends on the final production configuration, including which analytics events are enabled in the released application.

Firebase may process information according to Google's applicable privacy practices. For more information, visit: Firebase Privacy and Security .

7. Information We Do Not Provide Through the Game

Pen Cricket is designed as an offline-focused game. It does not provide internet matchmaking, cloud multiplayer, online leaderboards, live events, real cricket teams, player rosters, betting, gambling, fantasy sports, or prize-based gameplay.

8. Children's Privacy

Pen Cricket is designed as a family-friendly game. However, the appropriate audience designation and advertising configuration should be determined according to the final Google Play target-audience declaration and production build.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy concern regarding the use of Pen Cricket, please contact the developer using the contact information below.

9. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored by the application remains on the device until it is deleted through the application's storage controls, device settings, or by uninstalling the application.

Information processed by third-party services such as advertising or analytics providers is subject to those providers' applicable retention policies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the information handled by the application and to use appropriate technical protections for data transmitted through integrated third-party services.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-Party Services

Depending on the production configuration, Pen Cricket may use third-party services including:

  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising
  • Firebase Analytics for analytics

These services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms. Their data practices are not controlled by this privacy policy.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application changes, when third-party services are added or modified, or when legal or regulatory requirements change.

When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
