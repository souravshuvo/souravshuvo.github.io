Mini Games · com.childhood.minigames

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Published by Childhood Technology Ltd

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Mini Games stores gameplay information locally on your device, including scores, virtual balances, energy, streaks, timers, pet progress, game preferences, last-active times, and optional ad bonus coins.

The app does not require an account and does not operate an app-owned server that receives this gameplay information.

You can erase locally stored gameplay information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising and Consent

Mini Games uses Google Mobile Ads to show banner, native, interstitial, app-open, rewarded, and rewarded-interstitial ads.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information, advertising identifiers, app set identifiers, and related device information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and store advertising consent choices before ads are initialized.

A Privacy options control is available in the app when the consent platform reports that it is required.

Rewarded ads are optional and provide virtual in-game coins only.

For more information, please read Google's Privacy Policy or manage advertising choices in My Ad Center .

3. Children

Mini Games is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed primarily to children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through an advertising service, contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

App-owned gameplay data remains on the device until the user clears app storage or uninstalls the app.

Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and the user's Google advertising and privacy settings.

5. Security

The Android app blocks cleartext network traffic. Advertising and consent providers use encrypted network connections.

No method of electronic processing is completely risk-free, but the app limits its own data handling to local gameplay state.

6. Accounts and Online Services

Mini Games does not require users to create an account or sign in to play.

The app does not operate an app-owned backend server that receives or stores the gameplay information described in this policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when features, service providers, or legal requirements change.

The effective date above will be updated when a revised version of this policy is published.
