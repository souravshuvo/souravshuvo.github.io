1. Who we are

Childhood Technology Ltd. provides Object Finder (Android package: com.childhood.objectfinder). In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Childhood Technology Ltd.

This policy covers the game, privacy-related correspondence with us, and this policy page. It explains our own handling of information and the advertising services integrated into the app.

2. Information handled

Game information stored on your device

The app saves your puzzle progress, story clues, scores, gameplay statistics, avatar selection, unfinished session, tutorial status, daily Field Notes, Memory Lens balance, assistance state, and settings such as music, sound effects, haptics, and reduced motion.

These saves support gameplay on your device. We do not operate an account system or a cloud game-save service, and the game does not upload these saves to a server operated by us. Your operating system or device provider may back up app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and consent information

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and applicable privacy choices. Depending on your settings, region, and the services used, Google may collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate general location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other applicable identifiers.
  • App and ad interaction information, including app launches, ad views, taps, and video interactions.
  • Technical diagnostics and performance information, such as app or SDK errors, startup timing, and device performance.
  • Privacy-choice information used to record and apply applicable consent or advertising preferences.

The app does not request precise GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone access for its gameplay. Approximate location inferred by an advertising provider from an IP address is different from precise device-location permission.

Information you send to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or other details you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive information or personal information about another person unless necessary for your request.

We have not integrated a separate analytics or crash-reporting service into the game. Advertising providers may nevertheless process interaction and diagnostic data as described above.

  • Provide the game: restore progress, continue unfinished puzzles, apply preferences, track daily goals, and prevent repeated claims within the saved game.
  • Support advertising: deliver ads, measure ad performance, maintain the ad service, and detect invalid traffic or fraud.
  • Respect privacy choices: determine whether ads may be requested and apply the choices offered for your region.
  • Respond to requests: handle support messages, privacy questions, and reports of problems.
  • Protect the service and meet obligations: address security issues, enforce applicable rules, and comply with lawful requests.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires it, our legitimate interests in responding to correspondence and protecting the service where permitted, and applicable legal obligations. Advertising providers explain their own processing and legal bases in their privacy notices. Using the game does not, by itself, replace any consent required by law.

4. Advertising and your choices

Object Finder may show app-open ads at eligible launch or resume moments. It also offers optional rewarded ads for a brief second look at the original puzzle scene. Watching a rewarded ad is not required for ordinary story progress or free puzzle retries.

Where applicable, Google’s consent interface presents advertising choices. When the app is required to provide an ongoing privacy entry point, you can find Privacy options in the game’s Settings and review your choices there. Available options depend on your location and the applicable privacy message.

You can also manage or delete your advertising ID through supported Android privacy or advertising settings. Menu names vary by device. Restricting personalization does not necessarily stop all ads or all processing needed for ad delivery, security, or fraud prevention. Core puzzle play works offline, while ads require connectivity.

For more information, visit Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information, and Google My Ad Center.

Advertising-related information is processed by Google and applicable advertising partners to provide and measure ads and prevent fraud. Email providers process correspondence when you contact us. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect people, our rights, or the security of our services.

We do not sell your local game saves or support correspondence. Advertising-related sharing may fall within broader legal definitions of “sharing,” “sale,” or targeted advertising in some jurisdictions; applicable choices and rights may be available through the privacy options described in this policy.

If you tap an ad or an external link, you may visit a service with its own privacy practices. This policy does not govern those third-party destinations. Google’s description of Mobile Ads SDK data handling provides additional information about the advertising SDK.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data: saves remain in the app’s device storage until you remove them, clear the app’s storage, or otherwise remove the installation’s data. You can generally clear them through Android Settings → Apps → Object Finder → Storage → Clear storage/data. This also removes saved progress and preferences from that installation. Device backups may remain or restore data according to your backup provider’s settings; manage those separately.

Support correspondence: we retain messages only for as long as needed to handle your request and any related follow-up, security, or legal obligations. Information is deleted when it is no longer needed for those purposes, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Advertising data: Google and other relevant providers apply their own retention and deletion practices. Clearing the game’s data does not automatically erase information already processed by those providers. Use their privacy controls or contact them for provider-held information.

To request deletion of correspondence or other personal information held by Childhood Technology Ltd., email shuvo4727@gmail.com. We may request limited information necessary to verify and process your request. We cannot remotely access or erase a save that exists only on your device. There is no game account to delete.
