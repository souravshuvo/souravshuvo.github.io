Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you play Sum Foundry or contact us.

Effective date: 24 October 2026
Last updated: 24 October 2026

At a glance: You do not need an account to play. Game progress is saved on your device. Advertising services process information to provide and measure ads. If you send a support message, we receive the information you choose to include.

1. Who we are

Childhood Technology Ltd. provides Sum Foundry (the “App”). This policy explains the information handled through the App, support communications, and this privacy policy page. We are responsible for the personal information we control. Our advertising and support providers also handle information as described below.

For privacy questions or requests, email shuvo4727@gmail.com.

2. Information the App handles

  • Local game information: Puzzle placements, unfinished boards, scores, unlocked content, tutorial state, daily challenge and commission progress, rewards, and sound, music, haptics, and motion preferences are stored on your device to operate and restore the game. The App does not upload these saves to a developer-operated account or cloud-save service.
  • Support information: If you use Contact support, the App sends your message and the App name through Formspree. We receive any personal details you put in that message. If you email us directly, we also receive your email address and the contents of your correspondence. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
  • Advertising and technical information: Google Mobile Ads may collect device or account identifiers, including an advertising identifier where available, IP address, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. Consent choices and related information are processed to manage advertising privacy preferences.

The App does not require registration and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location. It does not include a separate developer analytics or cloud account system. This does not prevent advertising providers from processing their own usage or diagnostic information.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to run the game, retain your progress and preferences, calculate daily content and rewards, respond to support requests, deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain service reliability, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as security and support, legal obligations, or your consent where required. You may withdraw consent for future processing without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Advertising and third-party services

Sum Foundry uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for app-open advertising and optional rewarded hints. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary puzzle play. Ad requests and the type of advertising available depend on your region, privacy choices, device settings, and service availability.

Google and advertising partners may use the technical information described above to serve ads, measure performance, and detect fraud. Advertising can involve device identifiers and other storage technologies; restricting personalized ads does not necessarily stop all technical data processing.

Read the Google Privacy Policy, Google’s explanation of advertising technologies, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Formspree processes messages submitted through the in-app support form. In addition to the submitted content, its service may process IP addresses and request metadata for delivery, operation, and abuse prevention. See the Formspree Privacy Policy. Direct email is processed by the email services used to send and receive it.

5. How information is shared

Information is shared with providers needed for the services described here, including Google for advertising and consent management, Formspree for in-app support delivery, and email providers for correspondence. Locally saved puzzle progress is not sent to these providers by our save system.

We do not sell your support messages or local game saves. Advertising providers’ use of identifiers for targeted advertising may be treated as “sharing” or a “sale” under some privacy laws; applicable choices are described below. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations.

6. Retention and deletion

Game information remains in the App’s local storage until it is removed, overwritten through normal use, or deleted through your device’s app-storage controls. Uninstalling ordinarily removes the installed App’s local data; operating-system backups or device transfers may retain or restore data according to your device and account settings. The App does not provide its own cross-device save synchronization.

We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to address your request, manage related issues, and meet legal obligations. When no longer needed, it is deleted or de-identified, subject to necessary backup and legal retention. Advertising and service providers apply their own retention practices.

You can request deletion of personal information we control by contacting us. Clearing local App data does not delete information already processed by Google, Formspree, or an email provider.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Advertising choices: Use Settings → Privacy choices when that entry is available to review the consent options offered in your region. You can also manage advertising identifiers and personalization through your device’s privacy settings and Google My Ad Center, where applicable.
  • Local data: Use your device’s app-storage controls to clear local game information. This resets saved progress and preferences. Review device backup settings separately if you also want to manage backup copies.
  • Support: Sending a support message is optional. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete correspondence we control.
  • Legal rights: Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising uses. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Send requests to shuvo4727@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and will respond as required by applicable law. We cannot identify or recover a local game save from an email address alone.

8. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required without that consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or another service connected with the App, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required. A parent or guardian may contact us on a child’s behalf.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled. In-app support submissions use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, where privacy laws may differ. Where required, international processing must be subject to applicable legal safeguards. The providers’ privacy notices describe their transfer practices.

10. This page and external links

This policy page contains no embedded advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts. When it is hosted online, its hosting provider may process ordinary access information, such as IP address and request time, to deliver and secure the page. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or our information practices change. The latest version will show its effective and last-updated dates on this page. We will provide additional notice or seek consent for material changes where required by applicable law.
