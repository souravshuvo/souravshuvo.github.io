1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy explains how Childhood Technology Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Color Courtyard mobile game and related support communications. We are responsible for the personal information we handle for these purposes and act as its controller where that term applies under local law.

Color Courtyard offers numbered coloring, Free color, saved artwork, progression, and optional rewarded hints. Core coloring can be used offline. Advertising, sending support messages, and some sharing destinations require a connection.

2. Information we handle

Game information stored on your device

The game saves your colored regions and unfinished pictures, completed artwork, tutorial status, collection progress, daily goals and claims, hint balance, ad-reward records, and preferences such as music, sound effects, haptics, and reduced motion. Local dates and timestamps help operate daily goals and reward limits. Recovery copies may be retained locally to help restore a damaged save.

These saves support gameplay and restore your progress between sessions. The game does not provide an account or a cloud-save service operated by us, and it does not automatically send your artwork to us. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device settings and its provider's policies.

Information you send to support

When you tap Send in the in-app support form, the game sends your message and the installed app's display name to our support service, Formspree. The app name is included in the message subject. The form does not require your name, email address, or phone number. Any information you type in the message is included, so please avoid passwords, payment details, and other sensitive information.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or other information you choose to send. Formspree and email providers also process technical delivery and security information, such as IP addresses and message timestamps, under their own policies.

Advertising and technical information

The Android version integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. These services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app or ad interactions, diagnostic information, and consent choices. The information processed depends on the service configuration, your choices, and applicable requirements. See the advertising section below.

The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location for coloring. It does not include a separate analytics or cloud-artwork service operated by us; advertising providers may still process analytics and diagnostics associated with their services.

3. How information is used

  • To operate the game, save and recover your artwork, and remember your settings.
  • To track goals, unlocks, hint balances, and earned rewards, including preventing duplicate claims.
  • To deliver, measure, and protect advertising services and manage privacy choices.
  • To respond to support requests, investigate problems, and improve reliability.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and protect the security and rights of players and our services.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as security and responding to support, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where required for advertising or related technologies. You may withdraw consent through available privacy controls without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Color Courtyard can display app-open and completion-break ads, as well as optional rewarded ads that provide a hint. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary coloring. Using the game offline prevents network ad requests while you remain offline.

Google and its advertising partners use information to deliver ads, measure performance, diagnose problems, and detect fraud. Ads may be personalized where permitted by your choices and applicable law. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve technical information needed to deliver and protect the service.

Where required, the game presents a privacy message before requesting ads. An Ad privacy choices entry appears in Settings when Google's privacy platform requires it. You can also review your device's advertising and privacy settings to manage available advertising-identifier controls.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure describes the SDK's data categories.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information is shared with service providers as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners: advertising delivery, measurement, diagnostics, fraud prevention, and privacy-choice management.
  • Formspree: processing and delivering the support message you submit. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Email providers: receiving and handling support and privacy correspondence.

When you use Share picture, the game prepares an image and opens your device's sharing interface. You decide which app or recipient receives it. The selected service handles that image under its own terms and privacy policy. Sharing is initiated by you; your artwork is not automatically published by the game.

We do not sell your artwork or support correspondence. Advertising-related disclosures may be treated as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under some privacy laws; available rights and opt-out controls depend on your location. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data: saves remain in the app's storage until replaced, cleared, or removed. On Android, you can remove local saves through your device's Settings → Apps → Color Courtyard → Storage → Clear storage, or the equivalent controls on your device. This removes local artwork, goals, hints, settings, and recovery copies. Uninstalling generally removes app-local storage, but operating-system backups and images you exported or shared may remain and must be managed separately.

Support correspondence: we retain messages for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence we hold by emailing shuvo4727@gmail.com. We may retain limited information when legally required or necessary for a legitimate purpose.

Provider data: Google, Formspree, your email provider, and services you choose for sharing apply their own retention and deletion practices. Clearing the game's storage does not automatically erase information already processed by those providers. We can assist with requests concerning information under our control; provider-held information may require a request through that provider's privacy tools.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can play core coloring offline, decide whether to send a support message or share artwork, manage available ad privacy choices, and clear local game data using device controls. There is no game account to close.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

To make a request, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com with enough information for us to identify the relevant correspondence or issue. We may need to verify your request using proportionate information. We cannot remotely identify or erase a device-only save through a game account, because the game has no account system. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

The game does not require players to create a profile or enter a name, date of birth, or contact details to color. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances that require parental consent without that consent. Children should avoid including personal information in support messages or shared artwork.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact shuvo4727@gmail.com so we can review the situation and take appropriate action, including deletion where required. Device parental controls and available advertising privacy settings can provide additional choices.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled through our services. The in-app support form uses HTTPS, and local saves use the app's device storage. No transmission or storage method can guarantee complete security. Protect your device and consider what you include in support messages or share with others.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those countries may have different privacy laws. Where required, applicable transfer safeguards must be used. Provider policies explain their international processing and safeguards.
