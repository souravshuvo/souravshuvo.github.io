This policy explains how Childhood Technology Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Domino Detour (the “game”) or contact us about it.

Your progress stays on your device. Playing does not require an account. Advertising services may process device and interaction information, and sending a support request shares the information you choose to provide.

1. About this policy

This policy covers the game and our related support communications. It also identifies third-party services used for advertising and support. Those providers describe their own processing in the policies linked below.

For privacy questions or requests, email shuvo4727@gmail.com.

2. Information handled

Game data stored on your device

The game saves puzzle progress, unfinished board placements, attempts, completed levels, tutorial status, rewards, workshop restoration, daily commission progress, unlocked hints and settings such as music, sound, haptics and reduced motion. Local backup and recovery copies may also be kept to protect your progress.

These game saves are not uploaded to a player account or a gameplay server operated by us. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device and backup settings.

Information you provide for support

If you use the in-game support form, it sends your message and an automatically generated subject containing the installed app’s name through Formspree. The form does not require a separate name or email field. Any personal information you include in the message is submitted with it. Formspree may also process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect its service.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. Please avoid sending passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal details.

Advertising information

The game uses Google AdMob. Depending on consent, device settings and the advertising services involved, Google and its advertising partners may process identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This processing is separate from the local puzzle save.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location for gameplay. It does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or account system; advertising providers may perform their own measurement.

3. How information is used

  • To remember your progress, preferences and earned rewards.
  • To operate daily commissions, restoration and optional hint unlocks.
  • To respond to support questions and investigate reported problems.
  • To deliver and measure advertisements, manage privacy choices, and help prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and protect the service and its users.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on your consent, providing the service you request, legitimate interests such as support and security, or compliance with law, as applicable. Consent-based advertising choices can be changed as described below.

4. Advertising and your choices

Domino Detour may display app-open advertisements and offers an optional rewarded advertisement for route guidance. You can decline a rewarded offer and continue normal puzzle play.

Where required, Google’s consent service presents privacy choices before eligible advertising requests. When the service requires an ongoing entry point, Settings → Privacy options lets you review or change those choices.

You can also use your device’s advertising privacy controls and Google’s ad controls. Restricting personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent contextual ads, security processing or measurement.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google also publishes Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by these providers when you use the relevant features:

  • Google AdMob and Google’s advertising partners: advertising delivery, consent, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention, subject to the applicable settings and provider policies.
  • Formspree: delivering in-game support messages. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Email providers: handling support correspondence sent to or received by our contact email.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or where necessary to protect rights, safety or the security of the service.

Advertising-related disclosures may be treated as “sharing” or similar regulated processing in some jurisdictions. Available consent and opt-out choices depend on your location and applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data: saved data remains on your device until it is removed, for example by clearing the game’s app storage or uninstalling the game. Clearing app storage removes your local progress and settings. Device backups and restored copies are governed separately by your operating system and backup settings.

Support correspondence: we retain messages for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, handle related issues, protect against abuse or satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. We may need to retain limited information where the law permits or requires it.

Provider-held data: Google, Formspree and email providers apply their own retention practices. Clearing your local game data does not automatically delete information already processed by those services.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related sharing. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request about information we hold, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com and describe your request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and locate the correspondence. We cannot remotely identify or retrieve a puzzle save stored only on your device.

For information controlled independently by a provider, its privacy policy explains the available request and account-control options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

8. Children’s privacy

The game does not require players to submit personal details to play. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13, or under a higher age where applicable law requires parental authorization.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, email shuvo4727@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required. Parents and guardians should supervise support submissions and review device privacy settings.

9. Security and international processing

Support submissions use HTTPS, and local progress uses the device’s app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please do not include sensitive personal information in support messages.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Privacy laws may differ between countries. Provider policies describe their international processing and applicable safeguards.

10. External websites and this policy page

Advertisements and external links may open services with their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

This policy document contains no analytics scripts, advertising tags or forms. If it is served online, its hosting provider may process ordinary connection and access logs to deliver and secure the page.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the game, service providers or legal requirements. The updated version will show a revised date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.
