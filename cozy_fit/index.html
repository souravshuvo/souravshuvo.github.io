This Privacy Policy explains how Childhood Technology Ltd. ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with Cozy Fit (the "Game") and related support communications.

1. Information we handle

Game information stored on your device

The Game saves puzzle progress, unfinished arrangements, scores, stamps, unlocks, cosmetic selections, daily-goal progress, tutorial checkpoints, and sound, haptics and motion preferences. It also keeps local information needed to manage ad rewards and limits, such as reward receipts, timestamps and counters for gameplay and ad events.

These saves are not intentionally uploaded to a game-progress server operated by us. The Game does not provide its own account or cloud-sync service. Your device or operating system may include app data in backups according to its settings; those backups are controlled by the platform provider.

Information processed by advertising services

When the advertising SDK connects, Google and its advertising services may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, advertising and other device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The information processed depends on the SDK, device settings, ad configuration and applicable requirements. More information appears in the Advertising section below.

Information you provide when contacting us

If you use in-app support, the Game sends the message you enter and a subject containing the installed app's display name to Formspree. The support form does not require a name or email address, but you may include personal information in your message. Formspree may also process network and request information, such as an IP address and submission time, to operate and protect its service.

Unsent support drafts are held in the current app session rather than intentionally written to the game save. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message, any attachments you choose to send and related email metadata. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Device access

Core gameplay does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise GPS location. Internet access is used for advertising and support. This does not prevent an advertising service from estimating your general location from your IP address.

2. How information is used

  • To operate the Game, restore your progress and remember your preferences.
  • To manage puzzle rewards, cosmetic unlocks, daily goals and ad-reward eligibility.
  • To deliver and measure advertising, maintain service reliability and help detect fraud or abuse.
  • To respond to support requests, investigate reported issues and communicate with you.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and protect users, services and legal rights.

The Game does not include a separate publisher-operated analytics or crash-reporting service in its current implementation. Advertising and support providers may carry out their own measurement and diagnostics as described in their policies.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android Game uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Ads may appear when opening or reopening the Game and at certain completed-chapter transitions. You may also choose to watch a rewarded ad for a disclosed in-game benefit. Declining a rewarded ad does not disable other advertising or the SDK's associated data processing. An account is not required for ordinary play.

Google may use the information it receives to provide ads, measure performance, personalize advertising where permitted, and detect invalid activity. Advertising identifiers and similar technologies may be used in mobile apps. Turning off personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all ad-related data processing or remove ads.

For provider details and available controls, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information and My Ad Center. Google's Mobile Ads SDK data-disclosure documentation describes SDK data categories. Device and Google controls vary by platform, region and account.

This policy is a notice, not an advertising-consent request. Where separate consent or privacy controls are required, those requirements must be met independently of this page. The current Game does not offer an in-game advertising-consent or privacy-options screen.

4. Who receives information

  • Google AdMob and its advertising services: to provide, measure and protect advertising, as explained above.
  • Formspree: to receive and route messages submitted through in-app support. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers: to deliver and store support correspondence when you contact us or a support submission is routed by email.
  • Authorities or other recipients when necessary: where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect safety, prevent abuse or establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

We do not offer your local game saves or support messages for sale. Advertising-related disclosures may nevertheless qualify as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under some privacy laws. Your applicable rights and choices are described below.

5. Storage and retention

Local game data generally remains in app storage until you clear that storage or uninstall the Game, subject to platform backup and restore behavior. Clearing the data can permanently remove progress, unlocks, settings and unused rewards. We cannot recover a device-only save through an email request.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, follow up on issues, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Retention depends on the information and purpose; we do not promise a single fixed period for all messages. Third-party providers maintain their own retention and deletion practices, described in their privacy policies.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Local saves: use your device's app-storage controls to clear local data, or uninstall the Game. Manage device backups separately. These actions may be irreversible.
  • Advertising: review your device's advertising-ID and ad-privacy settings and Google's available advertising controls. Options vary and may not eliminate all advertising or provider processing.
  • Support: you can choose not to submit a support message. Avoid including information that is not needed to explain the issue.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email shuvo4727@gmail.com about personal information held in our support correspondence. Describe your request and provide enough information to locate the relevant message.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to receive a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or opt out of certain advertising-related sales or sharing. You may contact us to exercise applicable rights or complain to your local data-protection authority. We may need to verify a request proportionately and retain information where the law permits or requires it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Deleting local game data does not by itself delete information already processed by Google, Formspree or an email provider. For information a provider controls independently, its own request channels and policies may also apply.

7. Legal bases, where applicable

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide the Game or respond to a request, support legitimate interests such as service security and reliability, comply with legal obligations, or rely on consent where required. A legal basis depends on the particular processing and jurisdiction. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Merely reading this policy or using the Game does not replace any consent required by law.

8. Children's privacy

Cozy Fit is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where applicable. Please do not include unnecessary personal information about the child in your request.

9. International processing

Advertising and support providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, including the United States. Privacy laws may differ between countries. Refer to each provider's privacy policy for information about its processing locations and international-transfer safeguards. Contact us if you have questions about information you have sent to us.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled. In-app support requests use HTTPS, and local saves rely on the device's app-storage protections. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee complete security. Protect access to your device and avoid sending sensitive information in support messages.

This policy page itself contains no embedded advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or contact form. When the page is hosted online, the hosting provider may process ordinary access logs, such as IP addresses and request times. Following an external link or opening an email link involves the destination service or your email application.
