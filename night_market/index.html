1. Information handled

Game information stored on your device

The Game saves information such as scores, completed shifts, tips and purchases made with in-game tips, upgrades, selected dishes, achievements, daily quest progress, claimed rewards, tutorial progress, unfinished cooking sessions, and sound, music, haptics, and motion settings.

These saves support gameplay on your device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your saved cooking progress. Your device or operating system may back up or restore app data according to your own backup settings.

Information processed by advertising services

The Game includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. Depending on your device, settings, consent, and the services used, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or account identifiers available to the service;
  • App and advertising interactions, such as launches, ad views, taps, and rewarded-ad events;
  • Device, app, and diagnostic information used to assess performance, errors, and reliability; and
  • Privacy preferences and consent-related information.

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location for its core gameplay. Approximate location inferred by an advertising service from an IP address does not require GPS access.

Information you choose to send

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or other details you provide. Please send only information needed to explain your question or request.

2. How information is used

  • Local game data restores your progress, settings, rewards, and unfinished sessions and helps prevent duplicate reward claims.
  • Advertising services use information to deliver and measure ads, administer rewarded-ad events, maintain their services, diagnose technical issues, and detect fraud or abuse. Ad personalization depends on applicable rules, eligibility, and privacy choices.
  • We use support messages to respond, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy requests.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide the requested service, based on consent for relevant advertising activities, necessary for legitimate interests such as support and fraud prevention, or required by a legal obligation. Google's own policies explain the bases for its processing.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The Game may show ads, including optional rewarded ads and ads at eligible menu or app-opening transitions. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary cooking, practice, or free retries.

Advertising services may use advertising identifiers, local storage, cookies within advertising content, or similar technologies. The Game checks privacy information through Google's consent tools. Where required, you may receive a consent message before relevant ad requests.

When available or required for your region, open Settings → Privacy Choices in the Game to review or change your choices. You can also use your device's advertising privacy controls to manage advertising identifiers and personalization. Available controls vary by operating system and region.

Turning off personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or stop all ad-related processing. Services may still process information for contextual advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable requirements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Sharing and service providers

Advertising-related information may be transmitted directly to Google and its advertising partners when their services operate. We may receive advertising reports, such as ad performance and earnings. We do not send your locally saved cooking progress to an account or leaderboard service.

Email providers process support correspondence when you contact us. We may disclose information we hold where necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect rights or safety, or address fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your saved game progress or support correspondence. Depending on applicable law, some advertising-related processing may be considered sharing for targeted advertising. The advertising choices described above and the rights below may apply.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local saves: Game progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until removed. You can clear the Game's storage in your device settings or uninstall the Game to remove its active local data. This also removes your local progress. Operating-system backups may retain or restore copies; manage those through your device or backup account settings.

Support correspondence: We retain messages only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain necessary support records, and meet legal or security obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information we hold about you.

Advertising information: Google and other providers apply their own retention practices. Removing the Game does not automatically delete information already received by those providers. Use their privacy tools or contact them to exercise rights over information they control.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information; request portability or restriction of processing; object to certain processing; or opt out of targeted advertising or legally defined sale or sharing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To make a request concerning information held by Childhood Technology Ltd., email shuvo4727@gmail.com. We may need reasonable information to verify and respond to your request. We cannot retrieve a local game save from your device remotely, and requests about information independently controlled by Google may need to be directed to Google.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us at shuvo4727@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required. Advertising services may process device information as described in this policy; parents and guardians can review available device and advertising privacy controls.
