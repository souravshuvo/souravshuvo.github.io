Privacy Policy

Childhood Technology Ltd.

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Your puzzle progress stays on your device. Sudoku Lantern does not require an account. Advertising services may process device and usage information, and contacting support sends the message you choose to share.

1. Who we are

Childhood Technology Ltd. provides Sudoku Lantern, an Android Sudoku game (application identifier com.childhood.sudoku). In this policy, “we,” “us” and “our” refer to Childhood Technology Ltd. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game and contact us.

For privacy questions or requests, email shuvo4727@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores your current and suspended puzzles, entries, pencil notes, tutorial state, completion statistics, daily goals and claims, earned explanation credits, board choices, and sound, haptic and motion preferences locally. Backup and recovery copies of game saves may also be kept on the device to help recover damaged saves.

The game does not require registration and does not provide our own cloud account or cross-device progress synchronization. Its own gameplay code does not upload your puzzle boards, notes or saved progress to a company game server. Core puzzles can be played offline.

A small, temporary on-device diagnostic log records events such as starting a session, requesting a hint or an ad outcome. The game's own event log is not sent to an analytics backend. Advertising providers handle their own usage and diagnostic information as explained below.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Sudoku Lantern uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertising, including optional rewarded ads. When advertising services are used, Google and participating advertising partners may collect or receive:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers, such as Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, video views and ad responses;
  • technical and diagnostic information about the app, device and advertising SDK.

These services use information to deliver and measure advertising, maintain their services, detect fraud and abuse, and, where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements, personalize ads. Non-personalized or limited ads may still involve information used for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

The game uses Google's consent-management tools to request choices where required before requesting ads. When available or required for your region, you can revisit Settings → Privacy options. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in your Android privacy or advertising settings. The exact device controls vary by Android version.

Declining an optional rewarded-ad offer does not prevent normal puzzle play. Advertising requires internet access; ad availability depends on privacy choices and advertising inventory.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Advertising partners' processing is subject to their own privacy notices and applicable law.

4. Messages you send to support

If you use the in-game support form, we receive the message you enter and an automatically generated subject containing the app's display name. The form does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Include contact information only if you choose to share it; avoid passwords, payment information and other sensitive details.

Support submissions are sent over HTTPS through Formspree, which processes and routes messages to our support inbox. Formspree and the email service handling delivery may also process network and delivery information, such as IP address and submission time, for delivery, security and abuse prevention. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.

If you email shuvo4727@gmail.com directly, we also receive your email address, the contents of your email and any information or attachments you send. We use support information to handle your enquiry, investigate issues and protect the service from abuse.

5. Why information is used and shared

We use local game information to operate the game, restore progress, apply your settings and prevent repeated reward claims. Support information is used to handle messages and resolve issues. Advertising information is processed for the purposes described in Section 3.

Information may be handled by service providers involved in advertising, consent management, support delivery and email. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, security, or people from harm. The game does not send your saved Sudoku boards or notes to advertisers through its own gameplay code.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on providing the service you request, your consent where required, legitimate interests such as security and responding to enquiries, or compliance with legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to the device's storage behavior. Clearing storage removes saved puzzles, progress, credits and preferences and cannot be undone through an in-game account. Any separate device backups are controlled through the relevant device or backup service.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle enquiries, resolve related issues, maintain necessary records and meet applicable legal requirements. You may request deletion by emailing us. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or dispute-resolution reasons.

Google, advertising partners, Formspree and email providers retain information under their own policies and applicable obligations. Clearing the game's storage does not by itself delete information already received by these providers. You may use their privacy controls or contact them about information they control.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Play core puzzles offline and choose whether to use optional rewarded ads.
  • Review advertising choices through the available in-game privacy controls and Android advertising settings.
  • Choose whether to contact support and what information to include.
  • Remove local game information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; or withdraw consent. Contact shuvo4727@gmail.com to make a request. We may need enough information to locate the relevant correspondence and verify the request. We cannot identify or retrieve a local puzzle save from an account because the game has no such account system.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Requests concerning information independently controlled by Google or another provider may need to be addressed to that provider.

8. Children's privacy

Sudoku Lantern is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not ask children to submit personal information or create an account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

Advertising and consent requirements can vary by age and location. The applicable privacy choices and advertising-provider requirements still apply when advertising services are used.

9. Security and international processing

The support form uses HTTPS, and advertising services use their supported encrypted transport. Local saves use the app's device storage. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Our advertising, support and email providers may process information in countries other than where you live, including the United States. Their privacy notices explain their international processing and applicable safeguards.

10. This page and external links

This privacy-policy document contains no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or submission form. If it is hosted online, the hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and secure the page. External websites linked here have their own privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game, service providers or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will show a revised “Last updated” date. Where required, material changes will be communicated through an appropriate notice or consent request.
