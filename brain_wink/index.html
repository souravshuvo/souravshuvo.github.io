Privacy at a glance

  • No account is required to play.
  • Game progress and preferences are saved on your device.
  • Google Mobile Ads processes information to provide advertising.
  • Support messages are sent only when you choose to send them.

1. Who we are

Childhood Technology Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is the developer of Brain Wink. This policy covers the game and support communications relating to it. It does not replace the separate privacy policies of Google, Formspree, your device platform, or websites you choose to visit.

For privacy questions or requests, email shuvo4727@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Brain Wink saves information locally so you can continue playing and keep your preferences. This includes:

  • Puzzle progress, unfinished attempts, completed levels, chapter discoveries, and best move records.
  • Hint tokens, revealed hints, and daily goal progress and claims.
  • Tutorial completion and practice progress.
  • Language, sound and music levels, mute, haptics, reduced motion, and graphics preferences.
  • A support message draft that has not been successfully submitted.

Daily goals use your device’s local date. The game also keeps local recovery copies of save data and a limited temporary buffer of diagnostic event names. These game saves and diagnostic events are not uploaded to a gameplay analytics server operated by us in the current version.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on its settings. Those backups are managed by your platform provider, not a Brain Wink cloud-save service.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Brain Wink integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The game may show an ad when the app opens or on an eligible return to the menu. Watching a rewarded ad to earn an additional hint token is optional. Core puzzle progression and retries do not require watching a rewarded ad.

When advertising services connect, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location.
  • Device, app, advertising, or other identifiers, subject to device settings and the ad request configuration.
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, ad views, taps, and video interactions.
  • Technical and diagnostic information about the app, device, SDK performance, and errors.

This information supports ad delivery, advertising measurement, service operation, security, and fraud prevention. Google describes its processing and available controls in its Privacy Policy and information for apps and sites using Google services.

Current advertising controls: advertising services can initialize when the game opens, even if you do not select a rewarded ad. The current version does not include an in-app advertising consent form or a “Privacy choices” setting. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not itself stop advertising-related data processing. This policy is an explanation of those practices, not a substitute for any consent required by applicable law.

Where available, you can manage advertising identifiers and related preferences through your device’s privacy settings and Google’s ad controls. The available choices depend on your device, account, region, and the advertising configuration; they do not necessarily stop all ad-related data processing.

4. Information you send for support

If you use the in-game support form, the game sends your message and an automatic subject containing the app’s display name to Formspree, the service that handles the submission. The form does not require your name, email address, phone number, or an account. Anything you include in the message is submitted as part of that message.

Formspree and the services involved in delivering the message may also process network and request information, such as your IP address and submission time, for delivery, security, and abuse prevention. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy.

An unsent or unsuccessful draft stays on your device. A message may reach the provider even if the app cannot confirm delivery, for example after a connection timeout. Because the form does not require a return address, a personal reply through that form is not guaranteed.

If you email shuvo4727@gmail.com directly, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

5. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Save and restore game progress, rewards, and preferences.
  • Provide advertising and administer optional rewarded hints.
  • Receive and handle support or privacy requests.
  • Maintain service security, investigate technical issues, and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

The game does not currently include a separate remote gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service operated by us. Advertising providers may nevertheless collect their own interaction and diagnostic information as described above.

The game does not implement access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location. A general location may still be inferred by advertising providers from an IP address.

6. When information is shared

Information may be processed by Google for advertising, Formspree for support submissions, and email service providers for support correspondence. These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell your local game saves or support messages. We may disclose information we hold when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or service security. If our business or the game is transferred, information held by us may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Opening an ad or an external link may take you to another service. Its own privacy policy governs information collected there.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game information

Saved progress and preferences remain in app storage until that storage is cleared or removed. Clearing Brain Wink’s app storage removes the local save, including progress, hints, preferences, drafts, and recovery copies. Uninstalling normally removes local app storage, although your platform may retain backup copies or restore data on reinstall according to your backup settings.

Support correspondence

Support information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. Copies may exist in the support provider’s systems, our support inbox, and service backups. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold. Removing the app does not delete a message already sent.

Advertising information

Advertising information is retained according to Google’s policies and applicable controls. We do not control all provider logs or backup retention periods and cannot promise immediate deletion from every provider system.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Play without an account. No Brain Wink account registration is required.
  • Keep support optional. You can play without submitting a support message.
  • Decline rewarded ads. Optional hint rewards do not block ordinary puzzle progression or free retries.
  • Manage local data. Clear app storage using your device settings, and review your platform’s backup settings. Clearing data also removes your game progress.
  • Review advertising controls. Your device or Google account may offer advertising privacy settings. Those controls are separate from the game.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of information, restriction of processing, or to object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may have a right to withdraw it. You may also have a right to complain to a relevant data protection authority.

Send requests to shuvo4727@gmail.com, identifying Brain Wink and the request. We may need limited information to verify and locate records. We do not receive your local game save automatically and may be unable to associate advertising-provider records with you. Requests relating to information controlled independently by a provider may need to be made directly to that provider.

9. Children’s privacy

Brain Wink does not require players to provide a name, age, date of birth, or contact information to play. The current advertising integration is configured to request child-directed treatment and a general-audience ad content rating. These settings do not mean that advertising services process no information.

Children should not include personal or sensitive information in support messages. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact shuvo4727@gmail.com to request review or deletion of information we hold. Please provide only the details needed to identify the message.

10. Security and international processing

In-game support submissions use HTTPS. Local game data is kept in app-managed storage. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by us, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google, Formspree, and email providers may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Privacy laws and protections can differ between countries. Their policies explain their processing locations, safeguards, and international transfer practices.

11. This privacy policy page

This page contains no analytics scripts, advertising code, tracking pixels, embedded media, or third-party fonts, and it does not itself set cookies or use browser storage. If the page is hosted online, the hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and access logs, to deliver and secure the page. Following an external link opens a service with its own privacy practices.

12. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the game, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page. Additional notice will be provided where required by applicable law.
