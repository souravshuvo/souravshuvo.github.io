Dew Bubble

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Dew Bubble is a bubble-shooter game published by Childhood Technology Ltd. The game is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Dew Bubble does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, birth date, photo, voice recording, precise location, or other profile information.

Information Stored on the Device

Game progress, scores, stars, sound settings, and haptic settings are stored locally on the device.

This information is not uploaded by Dew Bubble. Users can erase locally stored game data from Parent Corner or by uninstalling the app.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app's data.

Advertising

Dew Bubble uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad requests use a maximum T content rating and request non-personalized advertisements.

On Android, the app requests access to the Android Advertising ID for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Android ad-services interest and attribution APIs remain disabled.

Google Mobile Ads may still process information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising. Depending on Google's applicable SDK behavior, this may include an IP address, which can indicate approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and app or device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app-set identifier.

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Google's explanation of how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services is available at: Google's partner-services information .

Google's privacy policy is available at: Google's Privacy Policy .

Consent and Privacy Choices

The app requests updated privacy status through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before requesting advertisements.

A user can open Parent Corner and select "Advertising privacy choices" when Google requires an additional privacy-options form.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the standard production configuration.

The Play Store release described by this policy must be built with ANALYTICS_ENABLED=false.

Age Restriction

Dew Bubble is not directed to children under 13. The game is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Users can reset all locally stored progress from Parent Corner.

Data Sharing and Retention

Dew Bubble does not maintain a user account or publisher-operated player profile.

Locally stored game information remains on the device until it is erased through Parent Corner, the app's storage controls, or by uninstalling the app.

Information processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's applicable privacy, security, retention, and deletion practices.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be handled in accordance with applicable law and platform policy.
