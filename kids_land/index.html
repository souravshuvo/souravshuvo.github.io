Privacy Policy

KidsLand Preschool Games

Developed and published by Childhood Technology Ltd

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Childhood Technology Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates KidsLand Preschool Games ("KidsLand" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use KidsLand Preschool Games.

1. No Account Required

KidsLand does not require an account or login to use the App.

The current version does not provide child accounts or child profiles.

2. Local Gameplay Data

KidsLand stores gameplay progress locally on the user's device.

Local gameplay information may include progress and settings needed to allow the App to restore the player's experience.

The current version does not provide cloud synchronization or cloud-based gameplay progress.

3. Device Permissions

The current version of KidsLand does not request access to the following device features:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Location

KidsLand does not require these permissions for its gameplay activities.

4. Advertising

The production version of KidsLand may use advertising services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Advertising services may process information required to provide, measure, maintain, or improve advertising. Depending on the final app configuration, this may include device information, advertising identifiers, app interaction information, and other technical or diagnostic information.

The exact information processed by advertising services depends on the SDK configuration, device, operating system, and applicable Google services and policies.

Advertising configuration and the applicable Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be reviewed before the production release.

5. Analytics

The App may include Firebase Analytics integration.

Analytics functionality and its configuration may affect what information is processed. If analytics is enabled, Firebase may process information relating to app interactions, usage, device information, and technical or diagnostic information as described in Google's applicable Firebase documentation.

The final production configuration will be reviewed before release and reflected in the applicable Google Play Data Safety disclosures.

6. Children's Privacy

KidsLand is designed as a collection of preschool games.

The current store-listing configuration and target-audience declarations must be confirmed in Google Play Console before release.

If the App's target audience, advertising configuration, or other privacy-related configuration changes, we will review and update this Privacy Policy and the applicable store declarations.

7. Third-Party Services

The current app configuration may include third-party SDKs and services for advertising and analytics.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and service configurations.

The current KidsLand listing package does not describe account login, cloud synchronization, or payment functionality.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App and its associated services.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Gameplay progress stored locally remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user, the App's data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party advertising or analytics services is subject to the applicable third party's policies and retention practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party services, data practices, or legal requirements change.

If future versions add features such as accounts, cloud saves, purchases, crash reporting, or other online services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version of the Privacy Policy became effective.
