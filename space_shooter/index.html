Space Shooter

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Space Shooter is an arcade space shooter game by Childhood Technology Ltd.

The current version does not require an account or login. The game does not operate its own backend API and does not require users to provide personal information to play.

2. Local Game Progress

Space Shooter saves certain gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress and preferences can be maintained between sessions.

Locally stored information includes:

  • Best score
  • Best wave reached
  • Number of runs played
  • Sound settings
  • Haptic settings

This information is stored on your device using local application storage.

3. Advertising

Space Shooter uses Google Mobile Ads to show advertisements on non-gameplay screens.

Ads are not shown during active gameplay.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or use device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostic information, and related information as described by Google.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google's Privacy Policy

4. Analytics

Space Shooter may use Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay balance, session length, advertising performance, and retention.

Analytics events do not include names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or free-text personal content.

Firebase Analytics is enabled only when the required Firebase configuration is provided in the production build.

For information about Firebase's privacy practices, please visit:

Firebase Privacy and Security

5. Information the App Does Not Request

The current app does not request permissions or features for:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • SMS
  • Call logs
  • Health information

The current build also does not include purchases, crash-reporting software, or an app-owned backend API.

6. Children's Privacy

Space Shooter is not currently configured as a child-directed app.

The target-audience declaration and advertising configuration should be reviewed before publishing. If the app's target audience or advertising configuration changes, this Privacy Policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before release.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored by Space Shooter remains locally on the device until it is removed by the user.

Local application data can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

8. Consent

Where consent is required for advertising, Space Shooter uses or is intended to use Google User Messaging Platform consent handling before serving applicable advertisements.

The app respects applicable advertising consent choices.

9. Future Changes

If future versions add purchases, cloud save, login, crash reporting, online features, additional advertising partners, or other data-collecting services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data collection and its purpose.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, third-party services, or data practices change.

When this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same public URL with a new effective date.
