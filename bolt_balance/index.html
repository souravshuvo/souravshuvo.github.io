Privacy policy

This policy explains how Childhood Technology Ltd. (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use Bolt & Balance and contact our support team.

Effective and last updated: 24 October 2026

Your game progress stays in local saves. You do not need a game account. Advertising services process device and ad-related information, and support messages are sent only when you choose to contact us. The details and your choices are explained below.

1. Information the game uses

Local progress and settings

Bolt & Balance stores completed deliveries, move records, unfinished delivery checkpoints, tutorial progress, daily jobs, stamps, cosmetic selections and audio/accessibility settings in the app’s local storage. This lets you continue playing and keeps your preferences between launches.

We do not operate a game-account or cloud-save service, and the game does not upload these save files to our servers or attach them to support messages. Your device or operating-system backup service may include app data in backups, depending on your device and settings.

Information you send to support

If you use Settings → Contact support, the app sends the message you enter and the installed app’s name in the request subject through Formspree, our form-delivery provider. The in-app form does not require your name or email address and does not automatically attach gameplay saves, screenshots or diagnostic logs.

Anything you include in your message, such as a contact address, becomes part of that correspondence. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you send. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Formspree and email providers may also process network information, such as an IP address and request time, to deliver messages, operate their services and prevent abuse. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The Android game uses Google AdMob to show advertising, including app-open ads and optional rewarded ads. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay or free restarts.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. This can include:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device information and identifiers, such as an advertising ID or app-set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, ad views and clicks.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hangs and energy usage.

Processing depends on SDK configuration, your device, your privacy choices and applicable requirements. Turning off personalized ads does not necessarily stop processing needed to serve contextual ads, measure performance or prevent fraud. The game itself does not request precise-location permission.

Google’s User Messaging Platform manages applicable advertising privacy messages and choices. Where required, you can revisit them from Settings → Ad privacy choices. That entry appears when the privacy service indicates it is required.

Learn more in Google’s privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure.

3. How information is used and shared

We use local game data to provide gameplay, restore interrupted sessions, record progress and apply your settings. We use support correspondence to investigate problems, respond when contact details are provided, and handle privacy requests.

Advertising information is processed by Google and relevant advertising partners to deliver and measure ads, maintain their services and detect abuse. Support information is processed by Formspree and our email service providers to deliver and manage correspondence.

We may disclose information we hold when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect rights or safety, or prevent fraud and misuse. We do not sell your local game saves or support correspondence. Advertising-related disclosures may be treated as “sale”, “sharing” or targeted advertising under some privacy laws; see your choices below.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on providing the service you request, consent for advertising-related processing where required, legitimate interests in support and security, or compliance with legal obligations. Google explains its own legal bases in its privacy policy.

4. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Advertising choices: use the in-app Ad privacy choices entry when available. You can also manage your advertising ID and advertising preferences in your Android or Google settings. Available controls vary by device and system version. See Google’s advertising controls.
  • Local saves: you can remove app data through your device’s app-storage settings or uninstall the game. This can permanently remove your progress. Device backups may need to be managed separately.
  • Support: contacting us is optional. You may ask us to access, correct or delete correspondence associated with you.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising disclosures. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions.

Contact shuvo4727@gmail.com with your request. Include only the details needed to identify the information involved. We may need to verify a request before acting on it. We cannot retrieve local-only saves from your device, and requests concerning information held independently by Google or Formspree may also need to be directed to those providers. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains in app storage until it is removed, replaced during normal gameplay or deleted through your device settings. Daily job progress is replaced when the game advances to a new daily period; earned progress and owned cosmetics remain in your save.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, maintain necessary records or meet applicable legal obligations. We then delete or anonymize it where appropriate. Providers may retain information, logs or backup copies according to their own policies and legal obligations. Uninstalling the game does not automatically delete messages already sent to support or information already processed by advertising providers.

6. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. Support submissions are sent using HTTPS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Google, Formspree and email service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, where privacy laws may differ. Their policies explain their processing locations and applicable safeguards for international transfers.

7. Children’s privacy

Bolt & Balance is intended for teens and adults and is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13, or below the applicable age of consent where a different threshold applies.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at shuvo4727@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps to remove information we hold, subject to applicable requirements. Advertising providers handle their information under their own policies and applicable child-privacy requirements.

8. This page and external links

This privacy-policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party content, tracking pixels or cookies of its own. When the page is hosted online, its hosting provider may process normal web-request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, according to the hosting configuration and provider’s policy.

If you follow a link to another service, that service’s privacy policy applies to your visit. This policy does not describe data practices of unrelated websites or apps shown in advertisements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game, its services or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated policy here and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request your consent before a material change takes effect.
