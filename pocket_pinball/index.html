1. Information handled by the game

Local game data. The game stores scores, room and run progress, lives, tutorial checkpoints, records, daily contract progress and claims, signals, cosmetic ownership, selected finishes and audio/accessibility preferences locally. It also stores local information needed for ad eligibility and consent. Save backups and recovery copies may remain on the device. The game does not provide an account-based cloud save or upload its save files to a game server operated by us.

Your operating system or device backup service may back up app data according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by the relevant platform and your settings.

Support information. If you send a message through the in-game support form, we receive the text you submit and an automatic subject containing the installed app name. The form does not require your name, email address or telephone number. If you include personal information in the message, it becomes part of the support request. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you send. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Online service information. Advertising and support providers receive information necessary to communicate with your device, such as an IP address and request metadata. Advertising-related collection is described below.

2. Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. It includes an optional rewarded ad that can provide one extra life after an unsuccessful expedition, and eligible app-open ads at Home startup or return. Declining a rewarded ad does not prevent a free retry.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and app identifiers, including an advertising ID or app set ID where available and permitted.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information relating to the app and advertising SDK.
  • Consent selections and related information used to apply privacy preferences.

These services use information for ad delivery and measurement, SDK analytics, security and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising depends on applicable consent, device settings, region and service configuration. Non-personalized or limited advertising can still involve technical data processing.

Where required, a consent message is presented before advertising proceeds. When Google's consent system requires a privacy-options entry, Privacy choices is available in the game's settings or pause settings so you can review your selections. Changing a selection affects future processing as provided by that service; it does not automatically erase information already processed.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. You can also review Google's advertising information and controls.

3. Support and service providers

In-game support messages are sent over HTTPS using Formspree and delivered to our support team. Formspree may process the submitted message and technical metadata such as IP address and request time to deliver messages, prevent abuse and operate its service. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.

We use support information to understand and respond to requests, investigate problems and maintain necessary support records. A message sent without contact details may not allow a personal reply. We do not automatically attach your local save, device files or contact list to a support submission.

Service providers process information as described in their policies and applicable arrangements. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect people and services, or to establish or defend legal rights. A business transfer may involve relevant records subject to applicable protections.

4. Purposes and applicable legal grounds

We handle information to provide the game and saved preferences, fulfill requested support, provide advertising features, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in secure operation and support, legal obligations, or consent where required. You may withdraw applicable consent through the available privacy controls.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Play and save data: use your device's app-storage controls to clear local game data. This resets progress, settings and local recovery copies.
  • Rewarded ads: decline the offer and retry normally. Core gameplay does not require a rewarded ad.
  • Advertising preferences: use the in-game Privacy choices entry when available, and your device's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete an advertising ID where supported.
  • Support: choose whether to send a message and which information to include.
  • Privacy requests: email shuvo4727@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object or withdraw consent where those rights apply. Explain the request and provide enough information to locate any relevant support correspondence. We may need to verify a request before acting.

Rights and exceptions depend on your location and applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We cannot identify or remotely erase a save that exists only on your device, or directly control records held independently by an advertising or platform provider. We can help direct requests to the appropriate provider.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains until app data is cleared, it is replaced through normal use, or it is otherwise removed by the device or app. Uninstalling generally removes local app data, but platform backups may retain or restore a copy according to your settings. Review your platform's backup controls separately.

We retain support communications only for as long as needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. Information that must be retained for a legal or security reason may be kept for that reason. Provider-held advertising and technical records follow the relevant provider's retention and deletion practices.

No game account is created, so there is no game account to delete. Clearing local app data does not delete a support message already sent or data previously processed by an advertising provider.

7. Security and international processing

Support submissions use HTTPS, and Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts collected user data in transit. Local saves use app-local device storage and should not be treated as an encrypted vault. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Their policies describe international processing and applicable safeguards. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from those where you live.

8. Children's privacy

Pocket Pinball Expedition is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically designed for children. A general content rating is not a promise that online advertising or support services are suitable for every age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and, where appropriate, arrange deletion or other action required by law.
