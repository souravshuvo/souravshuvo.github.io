Color Sort

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Color Sort is a puzzle game published by Childhood Technology Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use the game.

Data Collection

Color Sort uses Firebase Analytics to measure gameplay events, such as level starts, level completions, restarts, invalid moves, undo use, and advertising-related events.

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or free-text personal information in order to play the game.

Local Game Progress

The app saves gameplay progress, unlocked levels, best moves, best stars, sound settings, and haptic settings locally on your device.

This locally stored game information is not sent to or stored on a server operated by Childhood Technology Ltd.

Advertising

Color Sort uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising. Advertisements are not shown during active puzzle play.

Depending on the app version and advertising configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information necessary for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

For more information about how Google handles information, please see Google's Privacy Policy .

Analytics

Firebase Analytics is used to understand how players use the game and to measure gameplay and advertising-related events. Analytics may be disabled automatically if Firebase is not configured in a particular build.

The publisher does not use Firebase Analytics to request your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Children

Color Sort is not currently configured as a child-directed app. The publisher's Google Play target-audience declaration and advertising configuration are intended to remain consistent with the actual distribution of the app.

If the target audience or advertising configuration changes, this Privacy Policy and the relevant store declarations will be updated before the applicable release.

Third-Party Services

Color Sort uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.
  • Firebase Analytics for app and gameplay measurement.

The current version does not include account login, payment functionality, cloud synchronization, or a custom backend server.

Data Sharing and Retention

Google and its applicable services may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

Locally stored game progress remains on your device until you delete the app's data, uninstall the app, or the operating system otherwise removes the data.

Because Color Sort does not maintain a user account or publisher-operated user profile, the publisher does not have a server-side player account database to delete.

Security

Information transmitted by Google and Firebase services is protected using encryption in transit where applicable. Third-party services are responsible for their own security and data-protection practices.

Your Choices

You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Available advertising and privacy controls may also be managed through your device, Google account, or applicable Google privacy settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, advertising configuration, analytics services, or other data practices change.

The latest version of this policy will display its effective date.
