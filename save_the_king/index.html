Save the King

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Published by Childhood Technology Ltd

1. Introduction

Save the King is an offline puzzle game. The current version does not require account creation.

The game uses Google Mobile Ads to show advertising on Android, subject to the player's consent where required by law.

2. Information the Game Stores on Your Device

The game stores level progress and best scores locally on the device so that play can continue after the app is closed.

This gameplay information is not sent to the developer or to third parties.

3. Children

The game does not ask children or other users to provide personal information.

Android advertising is configured for child-directed, G-rated requests. Google Mobile Ads may still process the information described below to deliver and measure advertisements.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Save the King uses Google Mobile Ads for advertising on Android.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, and ad-interaction data to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy.

The app requests and respects consent choices where the Google User Messaging Platform determines that consent is required.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google's Privacy Policy

5. Advertising Configuration

The app is configured to request child-directed, G-rated advertising.

Save the King does not include social sign-in, online accounts, or its own analytics.

If a future version adds an advertising partner, analytics, ads on iOS, or another data-collecting service, this Privacy Policy and the applicable store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Level progress and best scores are stored locally on the device.

Locally stored game information can be removed by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the application.
