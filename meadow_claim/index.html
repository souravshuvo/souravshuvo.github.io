Meadow Claim is provided by Childhood Technology Ltd. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Childhood Technology Ltd. This policy covers the game and communications you send to us about it.

For privacy questions or requests, email shuvo4727@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the game

Game saves and preferences on your device

The game stores progress and settings locally, including tutorial status, scores, wins, exploration, continent unlocks, scout selections, daily goal progress and claims, unfinished expeditions, and audio, haptics, and motion preferences. Local recovery copies help restore saved progress.

We do not operate an account or cloud-save service for this progress. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your device and backup settings.

Advertising and consent information

Meadow Claim uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. Depending on your choices, device settings, and the ad services used, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Advertising identifiers, app-set identifiers, and other device or account identifiers available to the advertising service.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, ad views, taps, and rewarded-ad events.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising software.
  • Your advertising consent choices and related consent records.

These services can process information even when an ad is not personalized. The game does not request access to your precise device location, contacts, camera, or microphone for gameplay.

Messages you choose to send

If you use Contact support, the game sends your message and the installed app’s name to Formspree, our form-delivery provider. The form does not require a name, email address, or phone number. Any personal information you include in the message is also sent. Formspree may process connection information, including your IP address, to deliver the request and protect its service.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you send. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in support messages.

  • Provide gameplay: save your progress, restore expeditions, apply preferences, and track local goals and rewards.

  • Provide support: investigate problems, respond when contact information is available, and maintain necessary support records.
  • Protect services and meet legal obligations: address abuse, security issues, and valid legal requests.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis relevant to the activity: providing a service you request, consent for processing that requires it, legitimate interests in support and service security, or compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent through the available controls; withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

  • Advertising choices: use Privacy options in the game’s Settings when that option is available. You can also use your device’s advertising controls to manage advertising identifiers and personalization. Available controls vary by device and region.
  • Optional rewarded ads: you can decline a rewarded-ad offer and continue using the game’s free retry options. Declining an offer does not by itself change your broader advertising consent settings.
  • Local progress: clear the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstall it to remove its local data. This can permanently remove game progress. Manage any operating-system backups separately.
  • Support information: contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain targeted advertising or legally defined sale or sharing. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Send requests to shuvo4727@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify and locate your request. Because gameplay saves remain on your device, we cannot remotely identify, retrieve, or erase those local saves. Requests concerning information independently held by Google or another provider may also need to be directed to that provider.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it, remove the app, or it is replaced through normal save operations. Uninstalling may not remove copies retained in device backups.

Support communications are retained for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or security obligations. When no longer needed for these purposes, information is deleted or de-identified. You may request earlier deletion, subject to applicable obligations.

Advertising and provider records are retained according to the relevant provider’s policies, your choices, and applicable law. Clearing game storage or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete information already held by advertising or support providers.

7. Security and international processing

Support submissions use HTTPS, and Google describes encryption in transit for Mobile Ads SDK data. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no device, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Your local saves also depend on the security and backup settings of your device.

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Privacy laws may differ between countries. Where legally required, appropriate safeguards must apply to international transfers; provider policies explain their transfer practices.

8. Children’s privacy

Parents and guardians should review the game’s age rating and advertising features before allowing a child to use it. The game does not ask players to enter their date of birth or create a personal profile.

We do not knowingly seek personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact shuvo4727@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.
